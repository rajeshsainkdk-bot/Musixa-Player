Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Application: Musixa Player (Android)

1. Overview

Musixa Player is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our practices regarding data collection, device permissions, and monetization through Google AdMob.

2. Permissions and Data We Access

Musixa Player requires minimal permissions strictly necessary to provide high-quality music playback and audio equalization:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used solely to scan, index, and play audio files stored locally on your mobile device. Your private audio files are never collected, sold, or uploaded to our servers without your explicit action.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Allows background music playback and lock screen notification media controls.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream verified community songs and display ads via Google AdMob.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display playback controls in the system notification shade.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies. You can manage your ad preferences via Google Account settings.

4. User Generated Content & Copyright (DMCA)

Musixa Player respects intellectual property rights. Users uploading music to the community catalog represent and warrant that they possess necessary rights or licenses. If you believe your copyrighted sound recording is made available without authorization, please email our Grievance Officer for prompt takedown within 24 hours.

5. Data Security & Storage

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as passwords, banking credentials, or personal tracking cookies. User favorites and equalizer presets are stored locally on your device via Android shared preferences.
